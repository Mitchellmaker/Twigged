Support

Need help with Twigged, your microphone permissions or a purchase? Start here or contact the maker.

How do the six free saves work?

Twigged includes six successful saves. Failed, misunderstood or cancelled attempts do not use a free save.

How do I restore my lifetime purchase?

Open the Unlock Unlimited screen and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Where are my saved items stored?

Saved items are held locally by Twigged on your device. Deleting the app also removes its local app data from that device.
